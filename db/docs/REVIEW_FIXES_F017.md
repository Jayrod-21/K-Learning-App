## NEW findings

- **NEW-1 (NIT)** — `SwipeCarousel.tsx:146-152` + `:200`: if `setPointerCapture` were to THROW during the `'h'` axis lock (the catch swallows it, deliberately, for happy-dom), the gesture proceeds as `'h'` uncaptured — and `onPointerLeave` exempts `'h'`, so an off-element release could still strand the ref. In real browsers `setPointerCapture` on an active pointer inside a `pointermove` handler does not throw in practice, so this is theoretical. Cheap hardening if ever touched again: record whether capture actually succeeded and let `onPointerLeave` also end an uncaptured `'h'` gesture.
- **NEW-2 (NIT)** — `stats.ts` discards the rejection reasons entirely. A total outage renders five honest "No data yet" panels indistinguishable from a fresh user, with no `console.warn`/log of the underlying `ApiError`s. Honest-but-silent is the right prod UX for this personal app, but a dev-visible `console.warn` per rejected route would cost two lines and help future debugging. Follow-up ticket at most.
